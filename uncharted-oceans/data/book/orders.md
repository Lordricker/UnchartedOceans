# Your Orders

*Read this first.*

The owners have given you this ship and one task. **Carry her from the port where she lies to the port marked with a gold diamond on the chart in the cabin.** Everything you need is in the books on this shelf.

## The passage

1. **Study the chart** on the table in the cabin. Click it and it fills your sight. The **gold diamond** is your destination. Out on the water, a gold diamond on the horizon shows the way to it. It gives no distance: you work that out from the chart.
2. **Cast off** from the pier.
3. **Set the sails.** Their lines hang from pins at the rails by the masts. Ease a line out and the sail drops and sets. Turn the yards with the braces until the sails fill.
4. **Steer** with the wheel, by the compass in front of it, toward the diamond. A second compass stands on the chart table.
5. **Learn your speed** with the log and line: click the coil of rope on the poop deck by the stern rail, and count the blocks that go over the rail while the sand glass beside it runs.
6. **Sleep through the long hours.** Click the bed, say how many, and the ship holds her speed, and her heading too unless you leave the wheel over, in which case she curves. You wake early when a port is close, when a storm is closing in, at the edge of the chart, or when her keel touches the bottom. Work out the hours from your speed and the distance on the chart.
7. **Come in slowly** when the port is close. Line her up with the berth and put the lines ashore.

## What your hands can do

Walk with **W A S D**, run with **Shift**, jump with **Space**, look with the mouse. **Esc** frees the mouse.

**Left click works the ship.** Anything you can use is outlined in white when you look at it: the wheel, the ropes, the pump handle, the chart, the bed, the bookshelf, the coil of log line and its sand glass. Click it, or hold the button for the wheel, the ropes and the pump.

At the wheel the keys you may use are shown at the foot of the screen: **Q** the wheel amidships, **Z** hold the course, and **V** the compass. At the pier, **E** puts the lines ashore. To cast off, take the wheel and press **E**.

## Aloft

To see farther, climb to the **lookout barrel** on the main mast. Go to the rope ladder (the shrouds) at the side of the main mast, step up to it, and climb with **W**, with **S** to come down. Near the top of the ropes you are drawn over to the ladder up the mast, and near the top of that you go up through the hatch into the barrel. In the barrel you stand and look about. Press any key that moves you (**W A S D** or **Space**) and you are put back on the ladder to climb down.

## The other books

- *Of Sails and the Wind* and *Of the Wheel and the Course*: how to make her go, and steer, and how to read the weather.
- *Of the Log and Line* and *Of the Chart and of Sleep*: how fast, how far, and how long.
- *Of Docking and Mooring*: how to arrive.
- *Of the Compass and the Stars*, *Of Weight and Sinking* and *Of Crew and Hammocks*: the rest of the trade.
- *Of the Ship and Her Words*: every word a sailor uses, from bow and stern to belay. Open it whenever a word in these books is new to you.

## Your ship

*The brig, named part by part.*

Learn her words before you give an order. The **bow** is the fore end and the **stern** the aft end. **Port** is your left hand as you face the bow, and **starboard** your right. **Fore** means toward the bow and **aft** toward the stern. The pier lies on your port side when you come in.
